# Expense Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines the general process and rules for submitting and reimbursing business expenses. Travel-related expenses have their own specific limits and are governed by the Travel Policy, which takes precedence over this policy wherever the two differ.

## 2. General Rules

### 2.1 Eligible Expenses

Expenses must be reasonable, business-related, and incurred in the course of performing job duties.

### 2.2 Receipts

A receipt is required for any expense over $25. Expenses without a required receipt will not be reimbursed.

## 3. Submission and Approval

### 3.1 Submission Window

Expenses must be submitted within 30 days of being incurred.

### 3.2 Approval

All expenses require direct manager approval before reimbursement is processed.

## 4. Reimbursement

Approved expenses are reimbursed within 10 business days of approval, via the standard payroll reimbursement cycle.

## 5. Relationship to Other Policies

- Travel-related expenses (flights, hotels, meals while traveling): see the Travel Policy for specific dollar caps.
- Equipment purchases: see the Procurement Policy for approval thresholds.
