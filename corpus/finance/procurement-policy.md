# Procurement Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines the approval thresholds required to purchase goods or services on behalf of Nexora Technologies, including equipment such as laptops. It does not define equipment eligibility or specifications — see the Device Policy for that.

## 2. Approval Thresholds

### 2.1 Purchases Under $1,000

May be approved directly by the requesting employee's manager.

### 2.2 Purchases Between $1,000 and $5,000

Require department head approval in addition to manager approval.

### 2.3 Purchases Over $5,000

Require Finance approval in addition to department head approval.

## 3. Equipment Purchases

Laptop and equipment purchases follow the thresholds in Section 2, based on the total cost of the configuration requested. See the Device Policy for standard configurations and who is eligible to request equipment.

## 4. Vendor Requirements

Purchases over $5,000 must use a vendor from the approved vendor list maintained by Finance, unless an exception is granted.

## 5. Recordkeeping

All approved purchase requests must be logged in the procurement system with the approving manager, department head, or Finance approver recorded.
