# Travel Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines rules and limits specific to business travel expenses. It supplements the Expense Policy; where the two conflict on travel-specific limits, this policy governs.

## 2. Trip Approval

All business travel must be approved by a direct manager in advance of booking.

## 3. Reimbursement Limits

### 3.1 Airfare

- Domestic flights: reimbursed up to a maximum of $600 per round trip, economy class.
- International flights: reimbursed up to a maximum of $2,500 per round trip, economy or premium economy class.

### 3.2 Lodging

- Domestic hotel stays: up to $200 per night.
- International hotel stays: up to $300 per night.

### 3.3 Meals

Meal expenses while traveling are reimbursed up to $75 per day, itemized receipts required for any single meal over $25.

## 4. Submission

Travel expenses follow the same submission window and receipt requirements as the general Expense Policy (30-day submission window, receipts required over $25), but are subject to the caps in Section 3 above rather than the Expense Policy's general limits.
