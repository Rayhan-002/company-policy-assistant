# Attendance Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines expectations around working hours, attendance tracking, and leave due to illness. It does not cover annual (vacation) leave — see the Leave Policy for that.

## 2. Core Working Hours

Employees are expected to be available during core hours of 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM local time, regardless of whether they are working on-site or remotely.

## 3. Attendance Tracking

### 3.1 On-site Employees

On-site employees log attendance via the office badge system.

### 3.2 Remote Attendance

Employees working remotely under the Remote Work Policy log attendance via the internal time-tracking system rather than badge access. This is a tracking mechanism only and does not affect eligibility to work remotely, which is governed separately by the Remote Work Policy.

## 4. Leave Due to Illness

### 4.1 Notification

An employee who needs to take leave due to illness must notify their manager before the start of the workday, or as soon as reasonably possible.

### 4.2 Duration and Documentation

Illness-related leave of 3 or more consecutive days requires a medical certificate submitted to HR. This leave is tracked separately from, and does not draw down, an employee's annual leave balance under the Leave Policy.

## 5. Unplanned Absence

Repeated unplanned absences without notification may result in disciplinary action per the Code of Conduct.
