# Benefits Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy describes health and supplementary benefits available to eligible employees of Nexora Technologies. It does not cover annual leave — see the Leave Policy for that.

## 2. Eligibility

### 2.1 Full-Time Employees

Full-time employees are eligible for health insurance coverage beginning after a 90-day probationary period.

### 2.2 Part-Time Employees

Part-time employees receive prorated benefits coverage based on contracted hours, beginning after the same 90-day probationary period.

### 2.3 Contractors

Contractors are **not** eligible for any benefits described in this policy. See the Contractor Policy for the full list of benefits contractors do and do not receive.

## 3. Coverage

### 3.1 Health Insurance

Eligible employees receive medical, dental, and vision coverage. Premiums are partially subsidized by Nexora Technologies according to the current benefits schedule maintained by HR.

### 3.2 Retirement Contribution

Eligible full-time employees receive a company retirement contribution matching up to 4% of base salary.

## 4. Enrollment

Benefits enrollment occurs within 30 days of eligibility start, or during the annual open enrollment period.
