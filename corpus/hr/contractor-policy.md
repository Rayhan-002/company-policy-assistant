# Contractor Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines which company policies and benefits apply to independent contractors engaged by Nexora Technologies, as distinct from full-time and part-time employees.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all individuals engaged under a contractor agreement, regardless of engagement length.

## 3. Benefits Eligibility

### 3.1 Annual Leave

Contractors are **not** eligible for annual leave benefits under the Leave Policy. Time off for contractors is governed solely by the terms of the individual's contractor agreement.

### 3.2 Health Benefits

Contractors are **not** eligible for health insurance or other benefits described in the Benefits Policy.

### 3.3 Equipment

Contractors may be issued standard equipment under the Device Policy, subject to department head approval and the approval thresholds defined in the Procurement Policy.

## 4. Applicable Policies

Contractors remain subject to the Code of Conduct, the Conflict of Interest Policy, and the Acceptable Use Policy for the duration of their engagement, even though they are excluded from the leave and benefits policies above.

## 5. Engagement Terms

Contractor engagement terms, compensation, and termination conditions are governed by the individual's signed contractor agreement, not by this policy.
