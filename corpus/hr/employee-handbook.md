# Employee Handbook

## 1. Welcome

Welcome to Nexora Technologies. This handbook provides a general overview of employment policies. For authoritative, current details on any specific topic, always refer to the dedicated policy document — this handbook is a summary and is not updated as frequently as individual policies.

## 2. Employment Basics

### 2.1 Employment Types

Nexora Technologies employs full-time employees, part-time employees, and contractors. Benefits and entitlements differ by employment type; see the Contractor Policy and Benefits Policy for details.

### 2.2 Working Hours

Standard working hours are 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM local time, Monday through Friday, with a one-hour lunch break. Flexible scheduling may be arranged with manager approval.

## 3. Time Off

### 3.1 Annual Leave

Full-time employees are entitled to 18 days of paid annual leave per year. See the Leave Policy for accrual rules, carryover limits, and eligibility details.

### 3.2 Sick Leave

Employees who are unable to work due to illness should notify their manager as soon as possible. See the Attendance Policy for details on medical leave.

## 4. Conduct and Compliance

All employees are expected to comply with the Code of Conduct and the Conflict of Interest Policy. Violations may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination.

## 5. IT and Equipment

Employees are issued standard equipment on their first day. See the Device Policy and Acceptable Use Policy for details on equipment eligibility and acceptable use of company systems.

## 6. Getting Help

For questions not answered by this handbook, contact HR directly or consult the specific policy document relevant to your question.
