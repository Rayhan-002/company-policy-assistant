# Leave Policy (v2.0 — Archived)

> **This version is archived and no longer in effect.** It applied from 2025-01-01 until superseded by Leave Policy v3.0 on 2026-07-01.

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines annual (vacation) leave entitlements for eligible employees of Nexora Technologies.

## 2. Eligibility

### 2.1 Full-Time Employees

Full-time employees accrue annual leave at a rate that totals 18 days per calendar year.

### 2.2 Part-Time Employees

Part-time employees accrue annual leave on a pro-rated basis according to their contracted hours.

## 3. Accrual and Carryover

### 3.1 Accrual

Leave accrues monthly, at a rate of 1.5 days per month.

### 3.2 Carryover

Unused annual leave does not carry over to the following calendar year. Any unused balance is forfeited on December 31.

## 4. Requesting Leave

Leave requests must be submitted to a direct manager at least 5 business days in advance for approval.
