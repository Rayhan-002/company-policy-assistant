# Leave Policy (v3.0 — Current)

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines annual (vacation) leave entitlements for eligible employees of Nexora Technologies. This version supersedes Leave Policy v2.0 effective 2026-07-01.

## 2. Eligibility

### 2.1 Full-Time Employees

Full-time employees receive 20 annual leave days per calendar year, granted as a lump sum at the start of the year.

### 2.2 Part-Time Employees

Part-time employees receive annual leave on a pro-rated basis according to their contracted hours.

### 2.3 Contractors

Contractors are not eligible for annual leave under this policy. See the Contractor Policy for the full list of benefits contractors do and do not receive.

## 3. Accrual and Carryover

### 3.1 Grant

Unlike the prior version of this policy, leave is granted as a full-year lump sum rather than monthly accrual.

### 3.2 Carryover

Up to 5 unused annual leave days may be carried over into the following calendar year. Any balance beyond 5 days is forfeited on December 31.

## 4. Requesting Leave

Leave requests must be submitted to a direct manager at least 5 business days in advance for approval.

## 5. Change Summary from v2.0

- Annual leave entitlement increased from 18 to 20 days.
- Accrual method changed from monthly accrual to an upfront annual lump sum.
- Carryover introduced: up to 5 days may now roll over (v2.0 allowed no carryover).
