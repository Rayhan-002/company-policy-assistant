# Remote Work Policy (v1.0 — Archived)

> **This version is archived and no longer in effect.** It applied from 2024-01-01 until superseded by Remote Work Policy v2.0 on 2025-03-01.

## 1. Purpose

This policy governs where employees may perform their work.

## 2. Domestic Remote Work

Employees may work remotely within their home country with manager approval, on either a full-time or hybrid basis.

## 3. International Remote Work

International remote work is **not permitted** under this policy. All employees must perform work from within their country of employment.

## 4. Equipment and Security

Employees working remotely must use company-issued equipment and connect to internal systems only via the VPN described in the Acceptable Use Policy.
