# Remote Work Policy (v2.0 — Archived)

> **This version is archived and no longer in effect.** It applied from 2025-03-01 until superseded by Remote Work Policy v3.0 on 2026-07-01.

## 1. Purpose

This policy governs where employees may perform their work.

## 2. Domestic Remote Work

Employees may work remotely within their home country with manager approval, on either a full-time or hybrid basis.

## 3. International Remote Work

Unlike the prior version of this policy, international remote work is now permitted, subject to the following conditions:

- Maximum of 30 consecutive days per calendar year in any single country.
- Requires written manager approval submitted at least 2 weeks in advance.
- Employee remains responsible for any personal tax or visa implications.

## 4. Equipment and Security

Employees working remotely, domestically or internationally, must use company-issued equipment and connect to internal systems only via the VPN described in the Acceptable Use Policy.

## 5. Change Summary from v1.0

- International remote work is now permitted (previously prohibited entirely), capped at 30 days/year with manager approval only.
