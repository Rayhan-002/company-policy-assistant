# Remote Work Policy (v3.0 — Current)

## 1. Purpose

This policy governs where employees may perform their work. This version supersedes Remote Work Policy v2.0 effective 2026-07-01.

## 2. Domestic Remote Work

Employees may work remotely within their home country with manager approval, on either a full-time or hybrid basis.

## 3. International Remote Work

Employees may work internationally under the following conditions:

- Maximum of 90 days per calendar year, consecutive or cumulative, in any single country.
- Requires written approval from the employee's manager **and** HR **and** Legal, submitted at least 4 weeks in advance.
- Employee must file a Cross-Border Work Notification with HR prior to departure, covering tax, payroll, and immigration considerations.
- Employee remains responsible for any personal tax or visa implications not covered by the notification review.

## 4. Equipment and Security

Employees working remotely, domestically or internationally, must use company-issued equipment and connect to internal systems only via the VPN described in the Acceptable Use Policy.

## 5. Change Summary from v2.0

- International remote work cap increased from 30 to 90 days per calendar year.
- Approval now requires HR and Legal sign-off in addition to manager approval (previously manager approval only).
- A new Cross-Border Work Notification filing is now required prior to international remote work.
