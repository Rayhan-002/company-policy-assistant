# Device Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines who is eligible to receive company equipment and what standard configurations are available. It does not define purchase approval thresholds — see the Procurement Policy for that.

## 2. Eligibility

### 2.1 Full-Time Employees

All full-time employees are issued a standard laptop on their first day of employment at no additional approval required beyond onboarding.

### 2.2 Contractors

Contractors may be issued a laptop only with department head approval. The purchase or issuance still follows the dollar-based approval thresholds defined in the Procurement Policy.

## 3. Standard Configurations

Nexora Technologies offers two standard laptop configurations: a standard configuration (under $1,000) and a performance configuration (over $1,000, primarily for engineering roles). Requests for the performance configuration therefore trigger the department head approval threshold defined in the Procurement Policy.

## 4. Replacement and Upgrades

Equipment replacement due to damage or end-of-life follows the same eligibility rules as initial issuance. Upgrade requests above $1,000 require department head approval per the Procurement Policy; requests above $5,000 additionally require Finance approval.

## 5. Return of Equipment

All company-issued equipment must be returned upon termination of employment or contractor engagement.
