# Code of Conduct

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out the standards of conduct expected of everyone working at or on behalf of Nexora Technologies, including employees and contractors.

## 2. Core Principles

### 2.1 Integrity

Employees must act honestly and in the company's best interest in all business dealings.

### 2.2 Respect

Employees must treat colleagues, customers, and partners with respect. Harassment or discrimination of any kind will not be tolerated.

### 2.3 Confidentiality

Employees must protect confidential company and customer information, consistent with the data-handling expectations described in this section (Nexora Technologies does not currently maintain a separate standalone Data Privacy Policy document; data-handling obligations are covered here and in the Acceptable Use Policy).

## 3. Conflicts of Interest

Employees must disclose any potential conflict of interest. See the Conflict of Interest Policy for the full disclosure process.

## 4. Reporting Violations

Suspected violations of this Code of Conduct should be reported to HR or through the anonymous ethics reporting channel.

## 5. Enforcement

Violations may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination of employment or contractor engagement.
