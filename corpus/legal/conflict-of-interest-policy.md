# Conflict of Interest Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines what constitutes a conflict of interest and the process for disclosing one, supplementing the general expectations in the Code of Conduct.

## 2. What Constitutes a Conflict

A conflict of interest arises when an employee's personal interests could improperly influence, or appear to influence, their professional judgment or decisions on behalf of Nexora Technologies.

## 3. Outside Employment

Employees must disclose any outside employment or consulting arrangement, particularly with a competitor, customer, or supplier, before accepting it.

## 4. Gifts and Entertainment

### 4.1 Acceptable Gifts

Gifts from vendors, customers, or partners with a value under $100 may be accepted without disclosure.

### 4.2 Gifts Requiring Disclosure

Gifts valued at $100 or more must be disclosed to a manager and may need to be declined or surrendered to the company.

## 5. Disclosure Process

Conflicts must be disclosed in writing to HR or the employee's manager as soon as they are identified, using the conflict disclosure form.

## 6. Consequences

Failure to disclose a known conflict of interest is treated as a violation of the Code of Conduct.
